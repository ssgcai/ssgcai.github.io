A little preview of the seven dishes—tap any to see what’s in it.

  • Papaya Salad

    Shredded green papaya mixed with garlic, chili peppers, tomato, salt and lime juice, served with sliced cabbage.

  • Chicken Laab

    Minced free-range chicken breast mixed with salt, rice powder, lime juice, chicken bouillon, cilantro and green onions; served on lettuce and topped with cucumber.

  • Moke Gai

    Chicken mixed with fragrant herbs—often lemongrass and dill—then gently steamed, traditionally in banana leaves.

  • Naam Toke · Sliced BBQ Tri-Tip

    Sliced BBQ tri-tip tossed with green onions, cilantro, sliced jalapeños, lime juice, salt, chicken bouillon and rice powder; served on lettuce and topped with cucumber.

  • Soop Pak · Matsutake Mushroom

    A Lao-style warm vegetable and herb salad with matsutake mushrooms, aromatics and sesame.

  • Lao Der Homemade Pork Sausage

    Free-range pork with ginger, lemongrass, garlic, shallots, kaffir lime leaves and Lao Der house spices.

  • Lao Coconut Custard

    A tender, gently sweet custard made with coconut milk and eggs.
